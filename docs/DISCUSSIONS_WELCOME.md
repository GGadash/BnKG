# Welcome to B&KG / BnKG

Use Discussions for questions, ideas and experience reports. Use Issues for reproducible bugs; include app version, operating system, instrument model/firmware and steps. Remove private measurements, addresses, serial numbers and credentials before posting.

BnKG is **under development**. Information may be incomplete, delayed or incorrect; double-check readings and settings on the instrument. No manufacturer endorsement or measurement/regulatory compliance claim.
