# Roadmap

B&KG is under development. Current capabilities: live Dashboard/SLM/Frequency, local history/review/export, guarded controls, original-frame recording, FLAC capture/playback, external AAC/Opus/paired MP4 export and limited 2255 native totals.

Next evidence-dependent work: additional BKC profiles/time series, native GPS annotations and marker uploads, actual 2245 testing, longer recording/drift and clean-machine/display-DPI acceptance. Local coordinate text is not native GPS; paired media is estimated from PC receipt times.

Windows and Ubuntu packages are development releases. Do not infer certification or broader hardware compatibility. More developed/different editions may be found at [GGadash](https://github.com/GGadash/), including [HBKG](https://github.com/GGadash/HBKG). See [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
